ABOUT
I bring together deep technical expertise, extensive field and operational experience, and engineering leadership developed over around 16 years in the oil & gas industry. My experience spans corrosion management, materials engineering, inspection, cathodic protection and asset integrity, supporting complex operating facilities, pipelines and static equipment. As a Chartered Engineer (CEng), Fellow of IOM3 (FIMMM) and Fellow of the Institute of Corrosion (FICorr), I have built my career around solving real engineering problems and translating technical knowledge into practical solutions. My work has ranged from corrosion and integrity assessments and failure investigations to major integrity programs, engineering standards, contractor management and optimization initiatives delivering significant improvements in reliability, risk reduction and business value. I believe strong engineering is not only about technical knowledge. It is also about judgement, collaboration, leadership and developing others. Throughout my career, I have led technical communities, shared lessons across teams, mentored developing engineers and contributed to initiatives promoting engineering among the next generation. I value professional integrity, continuous learning and making decisions that are technically sound, practical and sustainable.

PUBLICATIONS

Corrosion and Vapor Corrosion Inhibitor

AZVITRIN Engineering & Construction Magazine

OCTOBER 2015 · ISSUE 17

Kenan Kerimov

This technical article discusses the fundamentals and mechanisms of corrosion and introduces Vapor Corrosion Inhibitor (VCI) technology as a method for corrosion prevention. It explains how VCIs provide molecular-level protection of metal surfaces, discusses their application and testing, and highlights potential uses in the oil & gas industry for protecting equipment, piping and other metallic components during operation, storage and preservation.
